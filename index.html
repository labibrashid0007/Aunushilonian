About Aunushilon Academy

Building a stronger foundation for the future since 1996.

Learn, Grow and Inspire

Aunushilon Academy is an educational institution located in Matamuhuri Upazila of Cox's Bazar District, Bangladesh. Since 1996, the academy has been committed to helping students develop academically and personally in a supportive learning environment.
